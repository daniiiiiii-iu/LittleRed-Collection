## Browse Subjects

This word cloud visualization showcases the aspects of each Little Red Riding Hood story that make it unique. These terms are based in the assumption that there is a wolf, a girl, and a grandmother as the three main characters. If one of these changes, such as having a boy as Red Riding Hood, that is noted by the term "Boy Riding Hood." Other pieces of the story such as themes and special features are also represented here.
Word size is determined by frequency and all words link to a corresponding collection search.

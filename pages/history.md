**History of Little Red Riding Hood**

Little Red Riding Hood can be traced back centuries ago. There is discussion in the literary research world of hints of the story beginning as far back as 1022. The more widespread starting point is the french version of the story which was written in 1697. Little Red Riding Hood reached English audiences in 1729.
The most popular origin point, where the modern day Little Red began to be formed, is with the Grimm Brothers in Germany as they wrote their version "Little Red Cap" in 1812. These versions all hold similarities but each takes a unique path on the details and morals portrayed. With one Little Red dying while another is saved, a path can be followed as each Little Red takes her own path through the woods (Reynolds). 

Little Red Riding Hood has managed to escape the pages of her story, flowing into digital media in kids cartoons and other media. She often is seen as more independent adn more capable of escaping the wolf in these more modern day retellings as her death because less and less frequent.
